**Full name:** Stacy Sakat<br>
**Cohort:** HPF Cohort

Wanjiku runs a fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market and has recorded 14 days of sales alongside rough stock quantities and reorder levels. She needs to understand whether the stall is growing, which days and products perform best, what to restock, and where to focus her effort. This analysis uses core Python to summarize sales, compare weekdays with weekends, classify daily performance, identify reorder alerts, and calculate product gross margins.

**How to run:** [Open the notebook in Google Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/github/sakatstacy-ops/plp-da-a1-gikomba-stall-analysis/blob/main/stall_analysis.ipynb)

## Top Three Findings

1. Total sales were **KES 46,450** across 14 days, averaging **KES 3,317.86** per day.
2. Week 2 sales were **KES 24,400**, up **10.7%** from Week 1's KES 22,050. Weekend daily sales averaged **KES 4,900**, which is **82.5%** above the weekday average of KES 2,685.
3. **Tomatoes** (18 kg on hand against a 25 kg reorder level) and **Sukuma** (9 kg against 15 kg) are due for reordering. Avocado has the highest gross margin at **42.9%**.
